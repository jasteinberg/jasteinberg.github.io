This post is in final revision and will appear at this address in the coming days.

It asks when a linear truth direction in a language model's activations is
recoverable at all, using a signal-to-noise reading of the difference-in-means
estimator: a random-direction null on both the decoding and the steering side, a
within-class-covariance diagnosis of the cases where the estimator collapses onto a
nuisance axis, and a rank-one correction that restores the sign of a steering effect.
A companion reader's map of the truth-probe literature will go live with it.
